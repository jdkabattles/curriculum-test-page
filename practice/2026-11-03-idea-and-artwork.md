# Daily practice — 2026-11-03

## Idea title (3C check)

"How to Read a Blog Post Like a Master Blogger: Answer, Hook, and Proof in 60 Seconds"

- Clear: a stranger knows the topic (reading a blog post critically).
- Clickable: one specific benefit (60 seconds, answer/hook/proof).
- Contextual: keyword front-loaded in plain language, no stuffing.
- The post must actually deliver the 60-second check it promises.

## Answer first

A master blogger reads a post in this order: the direct answer, then the hook,
then one proof check — if any of the three is missing, the post is either
hiding its answer, has no reason to keep reading, or cannot be trusted.

## Hook after answer

Ever finished a post and realised you still could not say what it actually
proved? That gap is usually the proof step, not the writing.

## H2 outline ("In this post")

- What would you keep, and what would you drop?
